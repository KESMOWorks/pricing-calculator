About the Pricing Calculator

The KESMO Pricing Calculator was created to make pricing products and services easier by combining costs, transaction fees, and target profit margins into one clear calculation.

Purpose

What this calculator does

Many people know what something costs to make, purchase, or provide, but are unsure what they should actually charge. Simply adding a percentage to the cost can overlook marketplace fees, payment-processing fees, fixed transaction charges, and the desired profit margin.

The KESMO Pricing Calculator estimates a selling price that accounts for those factors while also showing the resulting profit, markup, break-even price, and maximum discount before losing money.

Intended users

Who it is for

The calculator is designed for people who need a clearer way to compare costs, fees, selling prices, and profit goals.

  • Small business owners
  • Online marketplace sellers
  • Freelancers
  • Craft makers
  • Resellers
  • Local service providers
  • Anyone comparing costs and profit margins

The problem

Why it was created

Pricing decisions often involve more than simply adding a markup to a cost. Percentage-based selling fees, fixed transaction charges, and desired profit margins all affect the final price.

The goal of the KESMO Pricing Calculator is to perform those calculations automatically while also explaining what each result means.

Important information

Use estimates carefully

Every business is different. Taxes, refunds, shipping, discounts, supplier agreements, and local regulations may affect actual costs and profits.

Results provided by this calculator are estimates and should not replace professional financial, accounting, legal, or tax advice.

Always verify important business decisions independently.
