About This Project

Helping You Price With Confidence

The Selling Price Calculator was created to make pricing products and services easier by combining costs, transaction fees, and target profit margins into a single, easy-to-understand calculation.

What this calculator does

Many people know what something costs to make or purchase, but are unsure what they should actually charge. Simply adding a percentage to the cost often ignores marketplace fees, payment processing fees, and desired profit margins.

This calculator estimates a selling price that accounts for those factors while also showing the resulting profit, markup, break-even price, and maximum discount before losing money.

Who it is for

This calculator may be useful for:

  • Small business owners
  • Online marketplace sellers
  • Freelancers
  • Craft makers
  • Resellers
  • Local service providers
  • Anyone comparing costs and profit margins

Why it was created

Pricing decisions often involve more than simply adding a markup to a cost. Percentage-based selling fees, fixed transaction charges, and desired profit margins all affect the final price.

The goal of this calculator is to perform those calculations automatically while also explaining what each result means.

Important reminder

Every business is different. Taxes, refunds, shipping, discounts, supplier agreements, and local regulations may affect your actual costs and profits.

The results provided by this calculator are estimates and should not replace professional financial, accounting, legal, or tax advice.

Always verify important business decisions independently.

Feedback
