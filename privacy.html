Privacy Policy

A Simple, Private Calculator

The Selling Price Calculator is designed to work without accounts, databases, or the collection of your calculator entries.

Calculator entries

The calculator runs inside your web browser using JavaScript. The costs, fees, margins, and other values you enter are used to calculate results on your device.

This version of the website does not send those calculator entries to a database or save them for later use.

Reloading the page or closing the browser may clear the values you entered.

Accounts and personal information

You do not need to create an account, provide an email address, or submit personal information to use this calculator.

This version of the website does not sell personal information.

Website hosting

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Like most website hosting services, GitHub may process standard technical information needed to deliver and secure the website.

That information may include details such as an Internet Protocol address, browser type, device information, requested pages, timestamps, and diagnostic or security records.

The handling of information processed by GitHub is subject to GitHub's own policies and practices.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

This version of the Selling Price Calculator does not intentionally use advertising services, visitor analytics, tracking pixels, or account-based personalization.

The website does not intentionally place its own tracking cookies for the calculator.

The hosting provider may use technical storage or security measures as part of operating its service.

Copied results

The Copy Results button asks your browser to place a text summary of the visible calculation on your device's clipboard.

The website does not receive or store that copied text. After copying, you control where the information is pasted or shared.

External links

The website may eventually include links to websites operated by other organizations.

This privacy policy applies only to the Selling Price Calculator. Other websites may have different privacy practices and policies.

Future changes

This policy may be updated if the website later adds features such as analytics, advertising, contact forms, downloadable products, or other services.

Any important change should be reflected on this page so visitors can understand what information is collected and how it is used.

Policy date

This privacy policy is effective as of July 24, 2026.

Privacy reminder

Avoid entering confidential client information, payment card details, passwords, or other sensitive personal data into any general-purpose online calculator.
