Privacy Policy

The KESMO Pricing Calculator is designed to work without accounts, databases, or the collection of your calculator entries.

Calculator entries

The calculator runs entirely inside your web browser using JavaScript. The costs, fees, margins, and other values you enter are processed on your device to calculate your results.

This version of the website does not send those calculator entries to a database or save them for later use.

Reloading the page or closing your browser may clear the values you entered.

Accounts and personal information

You do not need to create an account, provide an email address, or submit personal information to use the KESMO Pricing Calculator.

This version of the website does not intentionally collect, sell, or store personal information entered into the calculator.

Website hosting

This website is currently hosted using GitHub Pages. Like most website hosting providers, GitHub may process standard technical information needed to deliver, maintain, and secure the website.

That information may include items such as your Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type, operating system, requested pages, timestamps, and diagnostic or security logs.

Information processed by GitHub is governed by GitHub's own privacy policies and practices.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

Version 1 of the KESMO Pricing Calculator does not intentionally use advertising networks, visitor analytics, tracking pixels, or account-based personalization.

The website does not intentionally place its own tracking cookies for calculator use.

Hosting providers may use technical cookies or security-related storage required for normal website operation.

Copied results

The Copy Results button asks your browser to place a text summary of the visible calculation onto your device's clipboard.

The copied information is not transmitted to or stored by KESMO. Once copied, you control where that information is pasted or shared.

External links

Future versions of this website may contain links to websites operated by other organizations.

This privacy policy applies only to KESMO websites. Other websites may have different privacy practices.

Future changes

This privacy policy may be updated if future versions of KESMO introduce features such as analytics, advertising, downloadable products, contact forms, or user accounts.

Significant changes will be reflected on this page.

Policy date

This privacy policy is effective as of July 24, 2026.

Privacy reminder

Avoid entering confidential client information, payment card details, passwords, or other sensitive personal information into any general-purpose online calculator.
